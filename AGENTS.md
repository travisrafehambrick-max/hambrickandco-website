## What this company is

Hambrick & Co. helps small businesses clean up messy operating handoffs, using tools they already have.

Primary offer: Lead Response and Estimate Recovery for local home services and trades (landscaping, HVAC, plumbing, electrical first).
- Unanswered call → within seconds, a text that knows the business, asks what the job is, offers a booking, puts it in front of the owner.
- Every unanswered estimate gets a timed, job-specific follow-up that stops the moment they reply.
