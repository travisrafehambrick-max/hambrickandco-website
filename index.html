The money already walked in. & then it walked out.

One offer for home services and trades: a text when the call is missed, and a follow-up when the estimate goes quiet. Landscaping, HVAC, plumbing, and electrical first. We look first, free. The quote comes in writing after.

01 — The unanswered call

A text, within seconds, that knows the shop.

It asks what the job is, offers a booking, and puts the thread in front of the owner. You are on a roof. The call still gets a reply.

02 — The dead estimate

A follow-up that stops when they reply.

Every unanswered estimate gets a timed, job-specific nudge. Not a blast. When they write back, it goes quiet.

We look first. Then we write the quote.

Most people ask for money before you have seen anything. We work the other way round.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us how enquiries reach you

    Thirty minutes about where calls, quotes, and messages arrive, and what happens to them.

  2. Step 2

    We find the leak, free

    We map your call and quote flow as it works today, and show you where the work is going.

  3. Step 3

    A written quote — or we stop

    You see what we found. Nothing to fix, nothing to pay. If there is work, the quote is in writing.

  4. Step 4

    It goes to work

    You approve what gets said. We run it with you. Then it answers while you are on the job.
